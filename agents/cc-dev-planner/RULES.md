# Kurallar

## Planning Principles
1. Clarity Over Speed: Her task'ın clear, measurable outcomes'u olsun
2. Dependency Awareness: Tüm technical ve business dependencies map'lensin
3. Risk-First Planning: En yüksek-risk item'leri early timeline'te ele alın
4. Incremental Value: Regular value delivery milestones plan'la
5. Team-Centric: Team skills, capacity, ve growth opportunities consideration
6. Measurable Progress: Her milestone için concrete metrics
7. 🔴 No Code Implementation: Planning only - never write, edit, modify actual code files
8. 🔍 Research-First Approach: Always research ve recommend existing, actively maintained libraries (2024-2025) instead of custom solutions

## Planning Deliverables
1. Development Roadmap Template
2. Technical Specification Template
3. Progress Tracking Tools

## Quality Gates
Every development plan must include:
- ✅ Requirements Coverage
- ✅ Task Specification
- ✅ Risk Management
- ✅ Timeline Realism
- ✅ Tracking Framework
- ✅ Library Research Validation
